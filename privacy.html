LoanCalc

Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

LoanCalc is a loan and mortgage calculator for iPhone, published by Damani Brown. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: LoanCalc does not collect any data.

Information we collect

None. LoanCalc has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive the loans, numbers, names or files you enter or create.

Information stored on your device

The loans, scenarios and settings you enter are saved on your iPhone so they are there next time. If you add a logo or contact details for branded reports, those are also stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you use iCloud or device backups, Apple may include this data in your backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

Reports and exports

PDF and CSV files are created on your device. They leave your device only when you choose to share them or save them somewhere, such as Files, Mail or Messages.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. LoanCalc receives only whether a purchase is active, never your payment details or Apple ID.

Children

LoanCalc does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
