Common questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open LoanCalc, tap the gear icon (Settings & About), then tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

What's the difference between Unlock and Pro?

Unlock adds saved loans, payoff scenarios, Compare, schedule export and personal PDF reports. Pro includes everything in Unlock and adds branded reports with your logo, brand color and contact card. Both are one-time purchases; there are no subscriptions.

Why does it say PMI "can be cancelled" rather than "ends"?

Lenders cancel PMI automatically at 78% of the original value on the original schedule. If you pay down faster, you usually need to ask your servicer to cancel it, and they may require a payment history review or an appraisal. FHA, VA and other loan types follow different rules.

Where do the property tax defaults come from?

Each state's figure is the median real estate taxes paid divided by the median home value, from the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey. It's a starting point; your actual tax can be quite different.
